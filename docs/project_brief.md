## 1. PROJECT BRIEF (project_brief.md)

### Game Title
**Divine Gestures: Babylon**

### High Concept
A mobile idle / light tower‑defense game where the player takes the role of a god. The player influences a living world by drawing divine gestures to cast spells, gain worship, and ultimately prevent humanity from completing the Tower of Babylon.

### Platform
- Android (Google Play)
- Portrait orientation

### Target Session Length
- 30–90 seconds per active interaction
- Offline progress between sessions

### Player Fantasy
> “I am a god whose power comes from belief, symbols, and subtle intervention — not direct control.”

### Unique Selling Points
- Gesture‑based spell casting (drawing symbols with finger)
- God‑scale perspective instead of controlling a unit
- Mythological endgame (Babylon)
- Offline‑first idle progression
