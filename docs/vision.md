## 2. VISION (vision.md)

### Design Pillars
1. **God, not General**  
   The player never directly controls units. All interaction is indirect, symbolic, and divine.

2. **Simple Actions, Deep Consequences**  
   A single gesture can alter belief, economy, and conflict chains.

3. **Idle, Not Absent**  
   The world progresses without the player, but always leaves meaningful decisions when they return.

4. **Readable Systems**  
   Every number and system should be explainable through world logic (faith, fear, devotion).

### Emotional Arc
- Early game: curiosity and experimentation
- Mid game: responsibility and control
- Late game: resistance, opposition, and god vs humanity conflict

### Endgame Theme
Humanity attempts to rival the gods by building the Tower of Babylon. The player must sabotage, influence, or overwhelm this effort using accumulated divine power.
