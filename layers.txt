Rozdelenie vrstiev:
	Layer 1: PlayerBody
	Layer 2: EnemyBody
	Layer 3: Walls
	Layer 4: PlayerHurtbox
	Layer 5: EnemyAttack

Player (God)
	CollisionLayer: 1 (PlayerBody)
	CollisionMask: 3 (Walls)
	(nebude kolidovať s enemy)

Player Hurtbox (Area2D):
	CollisionLayer: 4 (Hurtbox)
	CollisionMask: 5 (EnemyAttack)
	(detekuje enemy utoky)

Enemy (Human):
	CollisionLayer: 2 (EnemyBody)
	CollisionMask: 3 (Walls)
	(nebude kolidovať s playerom)


Enemy Attack Area (Area2D):
	CollisionLayer: 5 (EnemyAttack)
	CollisionMask: 4 (Player Hurtbox)
	(Takto sa budú Hurtbox a AttackArea navzájom “vidieť”)

Lightning projectile (Area2D):
	CollisionLayer: 6 (Lightning)
	CollisionMask: 2 (EnemyBody)
	(Takto sa budú Hurtbox a AttackArea navzájom “vidieť”)
